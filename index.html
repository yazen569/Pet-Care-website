Our mission

New pet owners are always welcome

Our mission at Pawsitive Care is to make professional pet care easy and welcoming for everyone, especially new pet owners. Whether you just brought home your first puppy, adopted a cat, or are still learning what your pet needs, we're here to help. No question is too basic. We explain every step, show you what we're doing and why, and share simple tips you can use at home.

Every dog and cat is welcome, whatever their breed, size, age or temperament. We go at your pet's pace, and yours.
