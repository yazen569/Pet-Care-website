Our mission

Grooming that meets every pet where they are

At Pawsitive Care, our mission is to create an inclusive, stress-free space where every dog and cat feels safe and cared for. Giant breeds and tiny ones, seniors and puppies, matted rescues and show coats, anxious cats and pets with disabilities all get the same patient, respectful care. We adapt our process to your pet, not the other way around.

🤝

All breeds & sizes

From Chihuahuas to Great Danes, Persians to Sphynx, no breed restrictions and no size surcharges for "difficult" coats.

🌿

Fear-free handling

Quiet rooms, breaks whenever needed, and low-stress techniques for nervous, reactive or first-time pets.

♿

Special needs friendly

Seniors, pets with mobility issues, blind or deaf pets, and those with medical conditions are welcome.

🐈

Cat-only hours

Dedicated cat appointments in a dog-free space, so our feline guests stay calm.

Vaccinations

  • Dogs
  • Rabies$25
  • DHPP (distemper combo)$35
  • Bordetella (kennel cough)$30
  • Leptospirosis$30
  • Cats
  • Rabies$25
  • FVRCP (feline distemper combo)$30
  • FeLV (feline leukemia)$35

What's included

  • Given by a licensed veterinarian during our vaccine clinic hours
  • Quick health check before each shot (weight, temperature, heart)
  • Signed vaccination certificate and rabies tag
  • Email or text reminder when the next dose or booster is due
  • Can be done on the same visit as a groom

Not included

  • Full wellness exams, diagnosis or treatment of illness or injury
  • Lab tests (heartworm, FIV/FeLV testing, fecal tests)
  • Prescriptions, flea/tick/heartworm medication and microchipping
  • Emergency care (please contact your vet or an emergency clinic)

Puppy and kitten series are priced per dose. Pets must be healthy on the day. We'll reschedule if your pet seems unwell.

Good to know

  • Who we serve: dogs and cats only, every breed, size, age and temperament.
  • Vaccine records: grooming pets need a current rabies vaccine. No records? We can vaccinate on site before the groom.
  • What we don't offer: sedation, medical treatment, surgery, boarding, daycare or dog walking.
  • Pricing: prices are starting estimates. Final price depends on coat condition and time needed, and we always confirm before we start.
