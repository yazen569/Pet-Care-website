Our mission

Grooming that meets every pet where they are

At Pawsitive Care, our mission is to create an inclusive, stress-free space where every dog and cat feels safe and cared for. Giant breeds and tiny ones, seniors and puppies, matted rescues and show coats, anxious cats and pets with disabilities all get the same patient, respectful care. We adapt our process to your pet, not the other way around.

🤝

All breeds & sizes

From Chihuahuas to Great Danes, Persians to Sphynx, no breed restrictions and no size surcharges for "difficult" coats.

🌿

Fear-free handling

Quiet rooms, breaks whenever needed, and low-stress techniques for nervous, reactive or first-time pets.

♿

Special needs friendly

Seniors, pets with mobility issues, blind or deaf pets, and those with medical conditions are welcome.

🐈

Cat-only hours

Dedicated cat appointments in a dog-free space, so our feline guests stay calm.
