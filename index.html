Our mission

Grooming that meets every pet where they are

At Pawsitive Care, our mission is to create an inclusive, stress-free space where every dog and cat feels safe and cared for. Giant breeds and tiny ones, seniors and puppies, matted rescues and show coats, anxious cats and pets with disabilities all get the same patient, respectful care. We adapt our process to your pet, not the other way around.
